### Key benchmark points
1. **AR process = 5 stages**: invoicing → proactive follow-up (remind BEFORE due date too) → cash application → dispute management (separate track!) → reporting/reconciliation.
2. **Dunning system needs 4 components**: trigger (day past due), cadence (email default, PHONE by day 14), escalation thresholds w/ defined OWNER per stage, exit condition (credit hold → demand letter → agency/legal). Standard timeline: 1-3d soft email; 7-10d follow-up email + dispute check; 14-17d phone+email; 21-25d escalation notice; 30d credit hold; 45d demand letter; 60+d agency/counsel. Calibrate to account RISK not just age.
3. **Failure modes**: no ownership per stage; sales interference (define pause policy); disputes buried in dunning queue (must EXIT dunning, separate resolution track); no consequence at end.
4. **Automate stages 1-3 only (emails), humans from stage 4** (call, hold, demand letter).
5. **Monitoring vs dunning**: watch payment-behavior drift (22d payer stretching to 35d = early signal) — prevention layer. (Our app HAS this via behavior profile + rating.)
6. **KPIs**: DSO, ADD (avg days delinquent), AR turnover, **CEI** (collected/collectible %, ≥80% good, ≤50% poor; CEI = (beginAR + credit sales - endAR)/(beginAR + credit sales - endCurrentAR)), Bad Debt Ratio, aging. Track together over time (trends).
7. **CEI drivers**: correct/timely invoicing, credit policy + customer segmentation w/ tailored terms, flexible payment formats, portfolio prioritization (call priorities! reactive vs proactive), reminders before due date.
8. **Gaviti best practices**: 2 emails before due date + 1 on due date + 3 after (14/30/45d); segment workflows by payment history/size; track email open rates; intelligent prioritization by risk+value; record ALL interactions (calls/emails/notes) centrally.
9. **Credit policy**: written rules, credit limits per behavior, anyone can look at account and know next action.
