# Customer architecture (agreed with Kostas, Aug 2026)

Three levels:
1. Identity: group + company. One source of truth (name, VAT, addresses, contacts,
   vessels, payment terms, credit limit, currency).
2. Customer hub page `/customers/:id` — the customer's home. Identity + key figures
   on top, module tiles below. Does no work itself.
3. Modules — separate workspaces, own URL/filters/actions:
   - Receivables  `/customers/:id/receivables`  (today's group page, RENAMED from Collections)
   - Financials   `/customers/:id/financials`   (placeholder)
   - Quotations   `/customers/:id/quotations`   (placeholder)
   - Orders       `/customers/:id/orders`       (placeholder)
   - Contracts / Vessels — link into existing screens

Rules:
- modal = look, page = work. Anything with filters/lists/record creation is a page.
- Identity modal for group AND company, clickable everywhere, read-only, with two
  buttons: "Customer card" (hub) and "Receivables".
- Group is the collection level; company is the invoicing level and appears as a
  filter inside modules.
- Naming decision: the workspace is called **Receivables** (not Collections/Collection Case).
